8e · 写作技能 BECAUSE

用 because 把“建议 + 理由”连成一句

改写前（两句）正确写法（一句）
Go in spring. It’s very hot in summer.
春天去。夏天很热。		Go in spring because it’s very hot in summer.
春天去吧，因为夏天很热。
Travel by bus. It’s cheap.
坐公交出行。很便宜。		Travel by bus because it’s cheap.
坐公交出行吧，因为很便宜。
Buy your tickets in advance. It’s a very popular place.
提前买票。那里很热门。		Buy your tickets in advance because it’s a very popular place.
提前买票吧，因为那里很热门。
You can take a boat trip. There are a lot of canals.
可以坐船游览。那里有很多运河。		You can take a boat trip because there are a lot of canals.
可以坐船游览，因为那里有很多运河。

because 后面是理由；because 前面不加逗号。

七、写作

8e：读 Dani 的旅行建议

Popular city trips: BANGKOK　by Dani, Singapore. Date of trip: 22–24 June
Bangkok is an exciting place for a short trip because there’s a lot to see and do! The BTS Skytrain is a great way to get around. It takes you to different and interesting parts of Bangkok.
Visit the Grand Palace in the centre of the city. Go on a trip down a canal to the Artist’s House Baan Silapin – there are puppet shows in the afternoon. Or spend time at the famous floating markets because they’re an amazing experience!
There are great cafés and restaurants and there are lots of street food stalls. Try the Chinatown area because the food is amazing!
And don’t miss the Art and Culture Centre!

Rio 的范文 ① · 课本 8e · 4–7

旅行建议：来我的家乡广州

课本 8e · 4–7

为一个你熟悉的地方写 2–3 段旅行建议，至少一条小贴士（用祈使句），最后检查拼写、标点和动词。

Guangzhou is my home town. It’s a great place for a short trip because the food is amazing!
广州是我的家乡。它很适合短途旅行，因为美食太棒了！

The metro is a great way to get around. It’s cheap and fast.
坐地铁出行很方便，又便宜又快。

去沙面岛看看，傍晚沿着珠江散散步。

别错过广州塔！

Try the dim sum at a local restaurant.
在本地餐馆尝尝点心。

Go in autumn because it’s very hot in summer.
秋天去吧，因为夏天太热了。

Rio 的范文 ② · Unit Test · 13

我在中国最喜欢的地方：桂林

Unit Test · 13

What’s your favourite place in your country? Say where it is, how you can travel there, what you can see and do, where the best restaurants are, your favourite month to visit and what clothes to pack. Write 60–80 words.

旅游网站征文：写你国家里最喜欢的地方——在哪、怎么去、看什么做什么、哪里吃饭、几月去最好、带什么衣服。60–80 词。

☐ 回答所有问题　☐ 60–80 词　☐ 一条 tip　☐ 检查拼写、标点、动词

My favourite place in China is Guilin. It’s in the south of China, near Guangzhou.
我在中国最喜欢的地方是桂林，它在中国南部，离广州很近。

You can travel there by fast train.
你可以坐高铁去。

You can see beautiful mountains and the Li River.
你可以看到美丽的山和漓江。

Take a boat trip on the river because it’s amazing!
去江上坐船游览吧，因为太美了！

The best restaurants are in the old town. Try the rice noodles.
最好的餐馆在老城区，尝尝米粉。

My favourite month to visit is October because it isn’t very hot.
我最喜欢十月去，因为不太热。

Pack a jacket and a pair of comfortable shoes.
记得带一件外套和一双舒服的鞋。
